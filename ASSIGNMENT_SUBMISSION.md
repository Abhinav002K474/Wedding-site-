# ✦ Internship Assignment Submission — Aurelius & Elena Wedding Experience ✦

## Project
**Aurelius & Elena — Written in the Stars**

An interactive, mobile-first, single-page digital wedding invitation designed as a continuous magical narrative rather than a conventional wedding website.

## Concept
The experience combines a dark-academia, celestial and magical visual language with tactile interaction. The visitor progresses through nine connected acts, where information is revealed as part of the story rather than presented as isolated website sections.

## Experience Flow
1. **The Arrival** — An interactive candle introduces the visitor and activates the atmosphere.
2. **The Invitation** — A tactile 3D envelope and fracturing wax seal reveal the invitation.
3. **The Two Stories** — A fluid split-mask interaction introduces the couple through interactive portraits.
4. **The Journey Map** — A celestial trail reveals milestones from the couple's story.
5. **Pensieve Memories** — A liquid-distortion memory gallery responds directly to pointer and touch movement.
6. **Sanctuary & Venue** — Wedding schedule, venue information and travel details are revealed in-world.
7. **The Astrolabe Clock** — A live countdown is presented as an interactive celestial mechanism.
8. **The Enchanted RSVP** — Attendance is handled through living calligraphy and a wax-seal submission interaction.
9. **Save the Date** — A constellation monogram forms the final reveal and supports calendar export.

## Interaction & Motion Principles
The project uses a consistent interaction language:

- **Scroll →** environmental transformation and progressive story reveal
- **Tap →** tactile response and magical reaction
- **Swipe/Drag →** fluid image distortion and direct manipulation
- **Reveal →** content emerges from objects and the environment
- **Transition →** scenes morph into one another instead of behaving like disconnected blocks

The photo experience uses SVG displacement and turbulence to create a fluid, liquid-like transition that responds to interaction.

## Technical Implementation
- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- HTML5 Canvas
- CSS 3D transforms
- SVG filters including `feTurbulence` and `feDisplacementMap`
- Pointer/touch gesture handling
- Web Audio API procedural sound synthesis
- `requestAnimationFrame` animation loops
- Native browser `.ics` calendar generation
- Responsive, touch-first layout
- Reduced-motion and accessibility considerations

The project intentionally avoids framework-heavy dependencies and external audio asset dependencies.
